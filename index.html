USWDGC 2026 · Concept Brief

Women's History
Check-in Display

Six poster panels at registration, anchored by the Delicate Arch.
Five pivotal moments in the history of women's disc golf, plus a Utah welcome.

Prepared for Matt Rothstein & the PDGA Women's Committee
From the USWDGC 2026 Local Organizing Committee · ElevateUT Disc Golf
What we're building

A women's history wall
at check-in.

Six 24"×36" vertical posters in the registration area at the Utah Cultural Celebration Center, alongside the 12'×13' Delicate Arch centerpiece. Players, families, sponsors, and press will pass through this space throughout opening weekend.

  • Five panels trace pivotal moments in women's disc golf history, 1979 → 2017.
  • One welcome panel closes the loop on the present moment — the 25th USWDGC, in Utah.
  • Three of the women in the historical panels (Sheryl Newland, Elaine King, Juliana Korver) are physically registered for USWDGC 2026 — the wall is alive, not a museum.
  • The May 4 LOC vote placed the women's history display under Matt Rothstein's stewardship; this brief is our concept proposal for that display.
A note on what you're looking at

These are proof-
of-concept mockups.

Every poster image in this deck was assembled with AI image generation as a way to communicate intent — composition, hierarchy, and the narrative arc of the panel system. They are not press-ready.

What's intentional: the brand discipline (USWDGC navy / teal / yellow), the layout grid, the headline copy, the body text, the source citations, the screening callout, and the panel-to-panel narrative.
What's a placeholder: every photograph and the typography rendering. Final art will be produced by a designer working in Suiza · Adobe Garamond Premier Pro · Roboto with properly licensed archival photography sourced through the Steady Initiative partnership.

We're showing our work transparently. Treat what follows as a concept brief, not a finished design.

Proof of concept
Panel 1 · 1979 · Organized advocacy

Call off the wolves.

The first organized victory for women in disc golf. Hero artifact: Sheryl Newland's actual February 24, 1979 letter to Bob Gardner.

Proof of concept
Panel 2 · 1983 · The breakthrough

Marie Jackson became the first FPO World Champion.

Hosts Tom Monroe and Levone Wolfe overruled Headrick's one-division policy. 21 women played. Jackson won. There has never again been a Worlds without women.

Proof of concept
Panel 3 · 1993 · Running the sport

Elaine King was appointed PDGA Commissioner.

A 3× FPO World Champion took the wheel of the entire association. Same year: Waterloo NWC debuts; Lynne Warren chairs the Women's Committee.

Proof of concept
Panel 4 · 1999 · The inaugural

Juliana Korver won by 35 strokes.

The largest margin of victory in this championship's history. Still. Hero artifact: the actual 1999 magazine spread with Juliana Bower at the top of the inaugural scorecard.

Proof of concept
Panel 5 · 2017 · Equity

"…the countless honors I have been denied, because I am a woman."

Valarie Jenkins-Doss departs Innova. What followed: 2020 equal payouts · Tattar's $125K salary · the first Grand Slam · still ~7% participation.

Proof of concept
Panel 6 · 2026 · Welcome

A Utah welcome to the women of the world.

Closes with the Tita Ugalde quote the LOC unanimously approved. Three living subjects from earlier panels named in the field. Presented by MVP Disc Sports.

In partnership with

The Steady Initiative.

USWDGC 2026 is screening No Girls Allowed: The Untold History of Women's Disc Golf + a panel discussion during opening weekend. The synthesis behind these panels draws directly from that documentary's research.

We're proposing the check-in display run as a co-presentation with The Steady Initiative. Every panel carries an "Original research from No Girls Allowed" credit beneath the USWDGC mark.

What the partnership unlocks

Licensed archival photography through Steady's source materials — PDGA archives, Disc Golfer Magazine, Inside Disc Golf, FLOW newsletter scans, primary correspondence — for the photo slots in panels 2, 3, and 4.

A real archival voice instead of placeholder imagery. The mockups in this deck use documentary stills only as a visual aide for this conversation.

A natural editorial home for the women in the panels who are physically attending — Newland, King, Korver — and an expanded panelist roster for the Friday screening.

What we'd like from you

Three asks.

  • Confirm the partnership framing. Is "in partnership with The Steady Initiative" the right language? Any logo, wordmark, or attribution requests for the on-poster credit line? Anything you'd want the PDGA Women's Committee to second on the credit?
  • Open the archival photo channel. For panels 2, 3, and 4 we need licensed archival photographs — Marie Jackson at 1983 Huntsville, Elaine King and the 1993 Waterloo NWC, Juliana Bower at 1999 Hornet's Nest. Steady's source archive is the natural channel; would you be willing to broker access?
  • Feedback on the structure. Is 1979 → 1983 → 1993 → 1999 → 2017 the right five? Anything you'd swap or add? The PDGA Women's Committee's perspective is welcome — this is a community artifact, not a unilateral piece.
What happens after sign-off

Designer takes it from here.

Once the structure and photo channel are confirmed, the project moves to a designer for final art — working in the official USWDGC type system (Suiza · Adobe Garamond Premier Pro · Roboto), placing licensed photography, and rendering at print resolution.

  • Designer brief: the full copy, brand spec, layout grid, and source notes are already written and live with the LOC. Designer takes the brief, not the AI mockups.
  • Print spec: 24×36 vertical, heavyweight matte stock, mounted on coroplast for the registration area.
  • Print lead time: 7–10 business days from final art to delivery.
  • Install: coordinated with Eric Erickson around the Delicate Arch centerpiece.

The mockups are the conversation, not the deliverable. The designer is the deliverable.
