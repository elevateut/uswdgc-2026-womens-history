# USWDGC 2026 — Women's History Check-in Display

A concept brief for six 24×36 vertical poster panels at the USWDGC 2026 registration area, anchored by Eric Erickson's 12'×13' Delicate Arch centerpiece. Five panels trace pivotal moments in women's disc golf history (1979 → 2017); the sixth is the Utah welcome.

**[Open the deck →](https://elevateut.github.io/uswdgc-2026-womens-history/)**

Prepared by the USWDGC 2026 Local Organizing Committee for **Matt Rothstein** and the **PDGA Women's Committee**, in proposed partnership with **The Steady Initiative**.

---

The mockups in this brief are AI-assembled proofs of concept. Final art will be produced by a designer working in the official USWDGC type system (Suiza · Adobe Garamond Premier Pro · Roboto) with properly licensed archival photography sourced through the Steady Initiative partnership.
